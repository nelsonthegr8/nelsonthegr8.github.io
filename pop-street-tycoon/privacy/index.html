Privacy Policy

Pop Street TycoonSupport

Effective November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information the Pop Street Tycoon app ("the app", "we", "us") collects, why, and the choices you have. The app is made by Nelson Brumaire, an independent developer. Questions: contact support.

In short: there are no accounts to create and we don't ask for your name, email or contacts. Your game progress stays on your device. The app shows optional ads through Google AdMob, connects co-op shifts through Unity Gaming Services, and uses Apple Game Center for leaderboards. Ads are only personalised if you allow tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (cash, shops, upgrades, skins, cards and settings) is saved inside the app on your device. We don't receive it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app offers optional rewarded video ads, served by Google AdMob. To deliver, limit and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location may be inferred;
  • device information (model, OS version, language) and app information;
  • ad interactions (ads shown, taps, completed views).

Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your ad choices

  • Tracking: iOS asks whether the app may track you. If you decline, ads are not personalised. Change it any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): the app shows Google's consent form before any ads load.
  • Watching ads is always optional.

Co-op shifts and friend trades (Unity Gaming Services)

To let friends join each other's shops, the app signs in anonymously to Unity Gaming Services, which assigns a random player ID (not linked to your name, email or Apple ID). While you play a co-op shift, game data such as your position, carried items, character and display name passes between the players through Unity's relay servers, which also see your IP address. Card trades with a friend during a shift are sent the same way. Unity's use of this data is governed by the Unity Privacy Policy.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, the app submits your weekly scores (cash earned and friends helped) to Apple's leaderboards, and may show your Game Center nickname to friends you play with. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

In-app purchases

The Pro Pass subscription is processed entirely by Apple. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details; we only learn that the subscription is active so its perks can be delivered to you.

Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message, and use it only to reply. We delete support correspondence when it's no longer needed.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its processing. Because we don't hold personal data about players ourselves, most requests are best handled through the choices above or directly with Google, Unity or Apple. You can also contact us and we'll help. We do not sell personal information.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if our practices change and revise the effective date above. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
