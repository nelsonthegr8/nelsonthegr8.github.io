Pop Street Tycoon

Run a whole street of shops — with your friends.

Support Privacy Policy

Brew coffee, flip burgers, shake boba and sell booster packs. Grab orders, stack them high and serve the line yourself, then hire staff, buy machines and open new shops all the way down Pop Street. Call a friend in for a co-op shift, trade cards with them, and ride along in your car to your next shop.

Features

  • Four shops to build and run: Coffee & Donuts, Burger Joint, Boba Bar and Card Shop
  • Co-op shifts: friends join your shop, work alongside you and earn a share of sales
  • Dining halls up to 50 tables, drive-thrus and Rush Mode
  • A card market with packs, grading, auctions and friend trades
  • Eight cars to buy, from the Bubble Pop to the Apex Aurora
  • Weekly Game Center leaderboards
