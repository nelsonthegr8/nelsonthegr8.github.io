Privacy Policy

Idle Trader TycoonSupport

Effective October 26, 2026

This policy explains what information the Idle Trader Tycoon app ("the app", "we", "us") collects, why, and the choices you have. The app is made by Nelson Brumaire, an independent developer. Questions: contact support.

In short: there are no accounts and we don't ask for your name, email or contacts. Your game progress stays on your device (and in your own iCloud, if you use it). The app shows ads through Google AdMob and reports crashes through Firebase Crashlytics; those services collect device and usage data as described below. Ads are only personalised if you allow tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (cash, businesses, cards, packs, settings) is saved in a file inside the app on your device. We don't receive it. If you're signed in to iCloud, a copy is stored in your iCloud account so you can restore it on another device; that data is held by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and is not accessible to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app offers optional rewarded video ads, served by Google AdMob. To deliver, limit and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location may be inferred;
  • device information (model, OS version, language) and app information;
  • ad interactions (ads shown, taps, completed views).

Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your ad choices

  • Tracking: iOS asks whether the app may track you. If you decline, ads are not personalised. Change it any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): the app shows Google's consent form before any ads load. You can review or change your choices later in the app under Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.
  • Watching ads is always optional.

Crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics (a Google service) sends a crash report so we can fix the problem. Reports contain the crash stack trace, device model, OS version, app version, free memory/disk at the time of the crash, and a random installation identifier. They don't contain your name or contact details. Reports are kept for 90 days. See Firebase's privacy information.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details; we only learn which item was purchased so it can be delivered to you.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your device (for example, when your offline earnings are full). They're created locally; no notification data is sent to us.

Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message, and use it only to reply. We delete support correspondence when it's no longer needed.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its processing. Because we don't hold personal data about players ourselves, most requests are best handled through the choices above or directly with Google. You can also contact us and we'll help. We do not sell personal information.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if our practices change and revise the effective date above. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
