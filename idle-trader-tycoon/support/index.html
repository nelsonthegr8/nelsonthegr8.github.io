Frequently asked questions

Is my progress saved?

Yes. The game saves automatically every few seconds and whenever you leave it. If you're signed in to iCloud, your save is also backed up to your iCloud account so it can be restored on a new device.

How do offline earnings work?

Businesses with a manager keep earning while you're away, for up to 8 hours. When you come back you can collect them — or watch an optional ad to double them.

Can I turn off personalised ads?

Yes. If you declined tracking when asked, ads aren't personalised. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Players in regions with consent rules can also use Settings → Ad Privacy Choices in the game.

I bought something — how do I get it on a new device?

VIP Pass, Golden Ledger and the Starter Bundle are tied to your Apple Account. Open the Shop (gold badge on the dashboard) or Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Time Warps are used up when bought, so they can't be restored.

How do I reset my game?

Settings → Reset Game. This can't be undone.
