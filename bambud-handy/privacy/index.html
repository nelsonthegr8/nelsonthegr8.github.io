Privacy Policy

BamBud HandySupport

Effective October 29, 2026

In short: BamBud Handy doesn't collect anything about you. There are no accounts with us, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and the developer runs no servers. The app talks to your own Bambuddy server and, if you choose, to Bambu Lab's services, and keeps its settings on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your Bambuddy server address and API key. The key is kept in the iOS Keychain, only on this device.
  • If you sign in to Bambu Cloud: the sign-in token Bambu Lab gives back (in the Keychain), your email address, and your MakerWorld name and profile picture. Your password is never stored.
  • App settings: alert choices, the build plate you last picked per printer, the last printer status seen (to avoid repeat alerts), an ntfy topic name if you set one up, and simple counts of how often you've used the app (to decide when to ask for a review or a tip).

None of this is sent to the developer. Deleting the app removes it.

Bambu Lab handles what it receives under its own privacy policy (on bambulab.com); your Bambuddy server is yours, so what it keeps is up to you. If you set up instant alerts through ntfy, your Bambuddy server (not this app) sends alert text to the ntfy server you choose.

Notifications

Print alerts are created on your phone from your printers' status. To check while the app is closed, iOS occasionally lets the app contact your Bambuddy server in the background. No push service run by the developer is involved.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
