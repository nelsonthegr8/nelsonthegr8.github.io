BamBud Handy

Your Bambuddy printers, in your pocket.

SupportPrivacy Policy

BamBud Handy is a free iPhone app for Bambuddy, the self-hosted manager for Bambu Lab printers. If your printers run in LAN-only mode, the official app can't reach them; BamBud Handy talks to your own Bambuddy server instead.

  • Live printers: progress, layers, time left, temperatures, AMS and camera, with pause, resume and stop.
  • Print from MakerWorld: search, pick a profile and plates, choose the build plate and match filament colours to your AMS. Projects are sliced on your Bambuddy slicer with the designer's settings.
  • Alerts when a print finishes, stops or needs you.
  • Made for the iPhone Duo: a two-pane layout when unfolded, a phone layout when folded.

Download on the App Store

You need a running Bambuddy server and an API key from it. BamBud Handy is an independent app, not made by or affiliated with Bambu Lab or the Bambuddy project.
